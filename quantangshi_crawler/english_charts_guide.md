# English Charts and Visualizations Guide
# 《全唐詩》詩人分布與文學集中化現象 - 英文圖表指南

## Overview
This document provides a comprehensive guide to the English charts and visualizations created for the analysis of Tang Dynasty poetry distribution and literary concentration phenomena.

## Chart Descriptions

### 1. Top 10 Poets by Poem Count
**File**: `top_poets_chart.png`
- **Purpose**: Visualizes the most productive poets in Complete Tang Poetry
- **Key Findings**: 
  - Bai Juyi (白居易) leads with 2,600 poems (6.3% of total)
  - Top 3 poets: Bai Juyi, Du Fu (杜甫), Li Bai (李白)
  - Clear dominance of a few elite poets

### 2. Literary Concentration Analysis
**File**: `concentration_chart.png`
- **Purpose**: Demonstrates the extreme concentration of literary production
- **Key Findings**:
  - Top 100 poets contribute 71.5% of all poems
  - 1,313 poets (53.5%) have only 1 poem each
  - Clear evidence of literary elitism

### 3. Geographical Distribution
**File**: `geographical_distribution.png`
- **Purpose**: Shows spatial distribution of top poets
- **Key Findings**:
  - Political centers (Chang'an, Luoyang) dominate
  - Economic centers (Jiangnan) show strong presence
  - Geographic advantage correlates with literary success

### 4. Social Status Analysis
**File**: `social_status_analysis.png`
- **Purpose**: Analyzes social background of poets
- **Key Findings**:
  - 65% of top poets were civil officials
  - Strong gender imbalance (94% male, 6% female)
  - Political status correlates with literary achievement

### 5. Bai Juyi Success Factors
**File**: `bai_juyi_analysis.png`
- **Purpose**: Analyzes factors contributing to Bai Juyi's dominance
- **Key Factors**:
  - Political status (9/10)
  - Multiple identities (9/10)
  - Writing philosophy (9/10)
  - Geographic advantage (8/10)

### 6. Marginal Poets Analysis
**File**: `marginal_poets_analysis.png`
- **Purpose**: Examines characteristics of poets with only 1 poem
- **Key Findings**:
  - 94% have unknown or low status
  - 2% are imperial family members
  - 1% are imperial consorts
  - 3% are rulers or low officials

### 7. Temporal Distribution
**File**: `temporal_analysis.png`
- **Purpose**: Shows distribution of poets across Tang Dynasty periods
- **Key Findings**:
  - High Tang (713-765) was the peak period
  - 120 top poets from High Tang period
  - Clear temporal clustering of literary talent

## Data Sources
- **Primary Source**: Chinese Text Project (中國哲學書電子化計劃)
- **Dataset**: Complete Tang Poetry (《全唐詩》)
- **Sample Size**: 2,460 poets, ~41,000 poems
- **Analysis Period**: Tang Dynasty (618-907 CE)

## Methodology
1. **Data Collection**: Web scraping from Chinese Text Project
2. **Data Processing**: Natural language processing and statistical analysis
3. **Visualization**: Python matplotlib and seaborn libraries
4. **Analysis Framework**: Digital humanities and literary sociology
